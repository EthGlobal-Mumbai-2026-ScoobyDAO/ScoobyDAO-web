## Agent activity widget

The Reports list includes a read-only discovery monitor backed by `GET /status` on the explicitly configured `NEXT_PUBLIC_REPORT_API_URL`. It follows the same HTTPS-or-loopback origin validation as the native Reports adapter, rejects redirects, and sends no credentials. It refreshes every 15 seconds while visible and reports worker heartbeat and generic queued/running job counts. A heartbeat is not evidence of a token scan. This endpoint does not provide current token, screening results or discovery events; the widget explicitly marks token-level activity unavailable and must not be represented as a live discovery timeline. Missing, invalid, stale and disconnected states do not fall back to demo fixtures.

Backend and discovery owners must first integrate the runner and agree a versioned, durable activity DTO for source state, candidate identity, per-concern outcomes, failures, timestamps and resume/reconnect semantics. The frontend can then show actual token-level activity without altering the four-concern gate or the human selection boundary. Do not expose credentials, infer a concern from a provider hint, or fabricate a completion percentage. This widget neither starts discovery nor modifies deployment, publication or World ID behavior.
